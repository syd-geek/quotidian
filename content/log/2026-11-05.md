These days I am enjoying reading an excellent book called Sophie's World by Jostein Gaarder. The book is one of the best introductions to Western Philosophy. It is a novel in which the main character Sophie a teenager who receives letters from a stranger and it starts a wonderful journey of discovering philosophy.
