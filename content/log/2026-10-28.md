After leaving my career as a programmer (Thanks AI), I developed few hobbies and one of them is woodworking. I am at level zero where I am just finding my way around tools and some basic techniques. My main learning source is YouTube videos and a lot of trial and error. One thing I realised after watching numerous videos about woodworking is that people are passionate about their workshop. Many spend countless hours building the workshop and the workshop itself becomes their main project. 

This website is something like a workshop. I have started with something very simple and after working on it for few days I feel that this website will be the project I will write about most for at least some time. 

So where am I at?

So far I have developed a system using VimWiki and [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) static site generator. 

I am not using any Hugo theme but crafting everything myself. I have found many awesome themes but decided to go no-frills on this site.

In the near future I see most of content here related to how this website is built. In a way that is the point here. I am not trying to prove anything to anyone but just having fun developing this personal website.

Let's see how this goes.
