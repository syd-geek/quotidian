Two days Patrick Stewart died and since then I have been thinking about his role as Captain Jean-Luc Picard in [Star Trek Next Generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek:_The_Next_Generation). That series has a special place in my life. It inspired me to get into computers. I remember watching the show on TV every week and eagerly waiting for new episodes. This was a time before streaming services and binge watching. While we waited for the next episode, there were discussions amongst friends about the previous episode.

Two characters were always my favourite. One was Data and the other of course the Captain. I don't know what it was but I would sit on the computer after watching every episode just playing around, finding and learning new things. Just geeking out.

Thank you Sir Patrick Stewart for inspiring to have a super awesome career journey as a programmer.

Patrick Stewart's autobiography Making it so is on my bookshelf and I plan to read it soon.
