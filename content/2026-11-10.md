Today I was thinking about those people who use to operate horse driven carriages in towns. How did they feel when automobiles started taking over as a mode of transportation. What did they feel? Betrayed, left out, left behind? The change, any change is scary for those who do not accept it. For those who embrace it, something wonderful happens. Both at physical and mental level. So embrace the change. It will come regardless of what you feel of desire. It it inevitable.
