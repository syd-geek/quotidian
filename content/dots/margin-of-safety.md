Plans depend on estimates, and estimates can be wrong. A margin of safety leaves
room for error so that an imperfect forecast does not turn a small mistake into
a failure.

The margin depends on what else could be done with the same resources, so it
connects to [Opportunity Cost](opportunity-cost.md). [Inversion](inversion.md)
helps identify the failures a buffer should protect against.

This is one example of a [Mental Model](mental-model.md) for handling
uncertainty.
