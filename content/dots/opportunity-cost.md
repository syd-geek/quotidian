Every choice uses resources that could have gone to something else. The real
cost of a decision is the value of the best alternative you give up, including
time and attention.

Considering [Margin of Safety](margin-of-safety.md) helps account for
uncertainty in the option you choose. [Inversion](inversion.md) can reveal the
hidden costs that make an apparently good choice fail.

This is one example of a [Mental Model](mental-model.md) for comparing choices.
