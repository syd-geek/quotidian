Good decisions start with knowing where your understanding is reliable. Work
from the boundary of what you know, and treat confident guesses outside it as
questions to investigate rather than facts.

Knowing your limits pairs naturally with [Inversion]({{< relref "inversion.md" >}}):
both help reveal risk before acting.
