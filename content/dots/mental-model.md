A mental model is a simplified way to understand how something works. Models
help us notice patterns, reason about choices, and anticipate consequences, but
each is only a lens: use several models when a decision matters.

The notes on [Opportunity Cost](opportunity-cost.md), [Margin of Safety](margin-of-safety.md),
and [Inversion](inversion.md) are examples of models for making decisions.
