Instead of asking only how to succeed, ask what would reliably cause failure,
then avoid those conditions. Reversing the question can expose neglected risks
and make a vague goal easier to act on.

Inversion works especially well with the [Circle of Competence]({{< relref "circle-of-competence.md" >}}):
knowing the limits of your understanding helps identify what could go wrong.
