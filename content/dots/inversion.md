Instead of asking only how to succeed, ask what would reliably cause failure,
then avoid those conditions. Reversing the question can expose neglected risks
and make a vague goal easier to act on.

Use [Margin of Safety](margin-of-safety.md) to guard against the risks inversion
reveals. Looking at [Opportunity Cost](opportunity-cost.md) can expose what a
decision sacrifices while preventing one kind of failure.

This is one example of a [Mental Model](mental-model.md) for improving
reasoning.
