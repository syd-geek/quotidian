Pick up a book. Any book. Read few pages. If you don't enjoy reading it then put it away and feel no remorse. However, if you like it then stay with it. Some of them may be interesting stories, and some may teach you something new.

Do this day after day.

After an year or so, you will see changes in yourself. You will meet this new person who thinks about the world and things in it with clarity and deeper understanding. The only downside is that the previous you will be gone forever and no effort will bring him/her back.

Welcome to awareness.

### My Rating System

I use a 7 start rating system. I am still working on the details of the system but until then, this is the gist of it.

<span class="book-rating">★★★★★★★</span> Life changing. The book gave me a new way of thinking

<span class="book-rating">★★★★★★</span>I learned something new and interesting

<span class="book-rating">★★★★★</span> Gave me insights I did not have

<span class="book-rating">★★★★</span> I remain undecided if it was a good read or bad

<span class="book-rating">★★★</span> Mediocre at best

<span class="book-rating">★★</span> Started but could not finish it

<span class="book-rating">★</span> To be placed in the garbage bin
